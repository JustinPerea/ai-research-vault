### 1. Memorizing Who, Knowing How: Benchmarking Personal and Procedural Memory for Persistent Assistants
- **Source:** arXiv (new)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20022
- **Authors/org:** Maosheng Qin, Qi Zhang, Mingxuan Xia et al. (8 authors)
- **Focus area:** personal + procedural memory / harness personal agents
- **What it is:** PraxisMemBench: harness-based persistent personal assistance where personal and procedural memory must co-evolve (skills are user-conditioned).
