# AI Research Digest — Monday, July 13, 2026

## TL;DR

- **Agent evaluation** — Tencent Hunyuan's **Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench** ([arxiv:2607.08964](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08964)) posts a floor-level ceiling: 46 tasks × 9 categories with dense per-checkpoint partial credit; the **best frontier model reaches only 15.2% at high reward thresholds; average across all models is 4.3%**. Cost per task is punishing (~10M tokens, ~85 minutes wall-clock avg). This is the first agent benchmark whose grading protocol explicitly distinguishes "made progress" from "solved" at scale — extends the Terminal-Bench 2.0 line with dense-reward instrumentation.
- **Context engineering / long-context** — **Self-Guided TTT for Long-Context LLMs** ([arxiv:2607.09415](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09415)) closes a landmine: TTT on **random spans hurts** LongBench-v2 accuracy, TTT on **oracle spans** substantially helps, and the model can pick its own evidence spans well enough to capture **up to 15% relative improvement** on Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507 and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct. Test-time training is span-quality-sensitive — self-guided selection is the load-bearing primitive.
- **Local & open models** — **Soofi S 30B-A3B** ([arxiv:2607.09424](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09424), Deutsche Telekom / German Industrial AI Cloud) — first sovereign, fully-open **hybrid Mamba-Transformer MoE**, 30B/3B-A, ~27T tokens, matches dense 14–27B on combined DE-EN benches, best English + German scores among fully-open models, tops Olmo 3 32B and Apertus 70B. Direct M5-Max local-fit (3B active + Mamba-hybrid inference-cache-near-constant).
- **Long-context evaluation** — **WILDTRACE** ([arxiv:2607.09328](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09328)) — 481 tasks from 214 real long-form documents (technical incident reports + literary narratives) where evidence trails **emerge from the document's own causal / temporal / narrative logic**, not planted needles. Seven Pearl-hierarchy patterns, no synthetic evidence. Companion piece to yesterday's PredicateLongBench and Jet-Long — the "no more needle-in-haystack" retirement continues.
- **Architecture / linear attention** — ETH Zurich's **Linear Attention Architectures** study ([arxiv:2607.07953](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07953)) head-to-heads DeltaNet / Gated DeltaNet / **Kimi Delta Attention** / Gated DeltaNet-2 at 350M–3B; **KDA + Muon wins validation loss**; introduces **Cross-Layer Value Routing (CLVR)** — forwarding the write value across layers — as a lightweight routing mechanism that lowers final loss.

## Items

### 1. Long-Horizon-Terminal-Bench: Testing the Limits of Agents on Long-Horizon Terminal Tasks with Dense Reward-Based Grading
- **Source:** arXiv
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08964
- **Authors / org:** Zongxia Li, Zhongzhi Li, Yucheng Shi, Ruhan Wang, Junyao Yang, Zhichao Liu, Xiyang Wu, Anhao Li, Yue Yu, Ninghao Liu, Lichao Sun, Haotao Mi, LeoweiLiang — Tencent Hunyuan
- **Focus area:** Evaluation / Coding agents / Long-running loops
- **What it is:** 46 tasks across 9 categories (experiment reproduction, software engineering, scientific computing, …) with **intermediate grading checkpoints that award partial credit** so incremental progress is measurable — not just terminal success. Frontier best is **15.2%** at high reward thresholds; **average is 4.3%**. Tasks cost **~10M tokens and ~85 minutes** on average per run.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** Dense-reward grading is what long-running agent training actually needs. Terminal-Bench 2.0 measures pass@1; this measures "how far did the agent get before falling off?" — which is the metric you optimize when the loop is autonomous and quiet failure ≠ zero progress. The **10M-token / 85-minute** budget also sets a realistic upper bar on what a single self-paced episode costs — useful sanity-check for MCP-tool loops. The gap between the 15.2% ceiling and 4.3% mean says the current frontier isn't "close but not quite"; it's **incrementally struggling across most task classes**.
- **Action signal:** `track` — read the task taxonomy before designing the next companion-agent long-horizon eval; wait for dataset release to inspect grading rubrics.

### 2. Self-Guided Test-Time Training for Long-Context LLMs
- **Source:** arXiv
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09415
- **Authors / org:** Xinyu Zhu, Zhe Xu, Xiaohan Wei, Yunchen Pu, Fei Tian, Chonglin Sun, Kaushik Rangadurai, Hua Zhi, Frank Shyu, Sandeep Pandey, Luke Simon, Yu Meng, Xi Liu
- **Focus area:** Context engineering / Long-context / Inference-time compute
- **What it is:** Test-time training on a **randomly sampled span** of a long context **hurts** LongBench-v2 accuracy; TTT on **oracle** evidence spans substantially improves it. Self-Guided TTT closes the loop: **the model identifies which evidence spans to learn from** before applying standard LM-loss training on those spans only. Delivers **up to 15% relative improvement** on Qwen3-4B-Thinking-2507 and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct across LongBench-v2 and LongBench-Pro.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** TTT is being repositioned as a **selective, model-guided operation** rather than a blanket adaptation — matches the direction of Rate-Distortion Compaction (arxiv:2607.08032, 2026-07-12) and Proactive Memory Agent (arxiv:2607.08716, 2026-07-11): the load-bearing bit is **which slice the agent chooses to internalize**, not the mechanism. For an M5-Max Qwen3-8B / Llama-3.1-8B stack this is directly runnable — 15% relative bump on hard long-context questions with no architectural change. Companion primitive for a companion-app's memory-refresh path.
- **Action signal:** `local-fit` — Qwen3-4B and Llama-3.1-8B both fit M5-Max comfortably; TTT-selectable evidence is a simple wrapper on top.

### 3. A Sovereign, Open-Source Foundation Model for German and English (Soofi S 30B-A3B)
- **Source:** arXiv
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09424
- **Authors / org:** Soofi-Team (30 members incl. Benedikt Droste, David Fitzek, Ruben Härle, Markus Frey, Daniil Gurgurov, Max Lübbering) — German Industrial AI Cloud / Deutsche Telekom, Munich
- **Focus area:** Frontier models / Local & open models
- **What it is:** **Sovereign, open-source Mixture-of-Experts hybrid Mamba-Transformer** — 30B total / 3B active per token, trained on ~27T tokens with German-language upweighting. Matches dense 14–27B on English+German combined benchmarks; **highest English AND German scores among fully-open models** — beats Olmo 3 32B and Apertus 70B. Superior code performance in both languages among 17 open models. Full release: weights + checkpoints + training code + data docs, permissive open-access.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** This is a **third fully-open hybrid-Mamba MoE at 30B/A3B** (after NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra 550B/A55B and Agents-A1 35B/A3B) — the class Justin can actually run locally. **3B active fits M5-Max Q4 in ~5–7 GB active weights with the Mamba-hybrid keeping KV cache near-constant across long contexts** — meaning a 64K+ session doesn't blow the KV budget the way pure-transformer 30B would. Full-openness matters for on-device federal work where data can't leave the box. Wait for GGUF / MLX conversion before benchmarking task quality.
- **Action signal:** `local-fit` — download weights once released; verify llama.cpp + MLX support before promoting to primary local base.

### 4. WILDTRACE: Benchmarking Natural Evidence Trails in Long-Context Reasoning
- **Source:** arXiv
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09328
- **Authors / org:** Zixin Chen, Peng Liu, Haobo Li, Rui Sheng, Jianhong Tu, Xiaodong Deng, Fei Huang, Kashun Shum, Dayiheng Liu, Huamin Qu
- **Focus area:** Evaluation / Context engineering
- **What it is:** 481 tasks from 214 real long-form sources (technical incident reports, lesser-known literary narratives) where **all evidence trails arise from the document's own causal, temporal, and narrative logic** — not planted needles. Seven distinct evidence patterns organized under Pearl's causal hierarchy, rigorous validation procedures.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** Pairs with PredicateLongBench (arxiv:2607.08284, 2026-07-11) and Jet-Long (arxiv:2607.07740, 2026-07-10) as the ongoing **retirement of needle-in-haystack**: the benchmarks that survive now measure "can the model reason over evidence that a real document actually contains" rather than "can it retrieve a planted string." For a companion-app or research-vault agent, WILDTRACE is closer to the actual task shape — dispersed evidence, causal chains, no planted signals.
- **Action signal:** `track` — read authors' evidence-pattern taxonomy; use as spec target for retrieval-eval design.

### 5. Towards Mechanistically Understanding Why Memorized Knowledge Fails to Generalize in Large Language Model Finetuning ("Knowing-Using Gap")
- **Source:** arXiv
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08393
- **Authors / org:** Lu Dai, Ziyang Rao, Yili Wang, Hanqing Wang, Hao Liu, Hui Xiong — HKUST
- **Focus area:** Fine-tuning / Interpretability
- **What it is:** Names the "**Knowing-Using Gap**" — models memorize new info during SFT but can't use it in downstream reasoning. Uses **self-patching** to track how memorized representations move through model layers and shows **memorized reps can exist internally but fail to route to computation-effective layers**. Diagnostic strategy **recovers 58–75% of the generalization gap** in failure cases.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** Explains a class of quiet failures Justin has seen in domain-tuned agents: the model "knew" the trained fact on eval-set recall but never surfaced it in downstream tool-use or planning. If self-patching-style routing metrics can be exposed as a fine-tuning-time signal, it becomes a pre-deployment check: "is this domain SFT going to actually be usable, or only recallable?" — cheaper than running the full agent-eval loop.
- **Action signal:** `track` — watch for a follow-up paper that turns the diagnostic into a training-time regularizer or an SFT reject signal.

### 6. Linear Attention Architectures: Mechanisms, Trade-offs, and Cross-Layer Routing
- **Source:** arXiv
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07953
- **Authors / org:** Tommaso Cerruti, Tim Rieder, George Rowlands, Lingfeng Jin, Imanol Schlag — ETH Zurich
- **Focus area:** Architecture / Long-context inference
- **What it is:** Head-to-head comparison of softmax attention vs four recurrent linear-attention families — **DeltaNet, Gated DeltaNet, Kimi Delta Attention, Gated DeltaNet-2** — under shared notation. **Kimi Delta Attention + Muon** wins validation loss at 350M; **pure Gated DeltaNet + AdamW** wins throughput; hybrid stacks trade throughput for loss. Introduces **Cross-Layer Value Routing (CLVR)**: forward the write-value across layers as a lightweight routing signal — lowers final validation loss for both DeltaNet and Gated DeltaNet. Runs to 3B on 15B tokens.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** First side-by-side of the four contending linear-attention families with a controlled apples-to-apples protocol. For local-inference planning: **KDA + Muon** is the empirical frontier on validation loss, **Gated DeltaNet** on throughput — pick the axis you care about. CLVR is architecturally simple (forward one signal), which means it's the sort of thing that could get retrofit onto existing checkpoints without a full retrain.
- **Action signal:** `track` — direct architectural feedstock for the next hybrid-attention release; not immediately actionable but material to any future model-choice call for local base.

### 7. CausalDS: Benchmarking Causal Reasoning in Data-Science Agents
- **Source:** arXiv
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08093
- **Authors / org:** Andrej Leban, Yuekai Sun — University of Michigan
- **Focus area:** Evaluation / Tools & connectors / Coding agents
- **What it is:** Bridges the gap between "symbolic causal-reasoning benches without real data analysis" and "data-analysis benches without causal structure" — every benchmark instance ships **a structural causal model + observational data + grounded natural-language narrative + empirically-informed real distributions**. Covers all three Pearl levels; **treats abstention (recognizing an unanswerable question) as a first-class scored outcome**; jointly evaluates symbolic causal reasoning, data science, uncertainty quantification, abstention, and tool-use / coding.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** Abstention-as-scored-outcome is the missing thing in most agent evals — this is the second reference-benchmark this month (after Two Axes of Abstention, arxiv:2607.08456, 2026-07-10) making "the answer is 'I don't know'" first-class. For federal data engineering that's not a luxury; it's the whole safety story. CausalDS also gives tool-use accuracy a joint-eval context: you can't score correctness of the answer without also scoring whether the tool call was warranted. Direct fit for auditing companion-app answer quality on tabular / analytical tasks.
- **Action signal:** `compare` — extends Two Axes of Abstention (arxiv:2607.08456) into task-typed multi-competency scoring, and complements Reason-Less-Verify-More (arxiv:2607.07405) pre-execution gating on the eval side.

## Conflicts & open questions

Cross-referenced against `_meta/claims-ledger.md`:

- **Long-context benchmark saturation trajectory** — WILDTRACE ([arxiv:2607.09328](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09328)) + PredicateLongBench ([arxiv:2607.08284](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08284), 2026-07-11) both explicitly abandon planted-needle evaluation. Frontier models degrade cleanly along the difficulty axes in both. **Open question:** Does Jet-Long ([arxiv:2607.07740](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07740), 2026-07-10) — which posts +4.79 / +2.18 / +2.03 RULER gains — hold those gains on WILDTRACE-natural-evidence, or is RULER-style eval getting outrun by the naturalistic benchmarks?
- **Test-time training vs. rate-distortion compaction** — Self-Guided TTT ([arxiv:2607.09415](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09415)) says "let the model pick which spans to internalize"; Rate-Distortion Memory Compaction ([arxiv:2607.08032](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.08032), 2026-07-12) says "attention-magnitude / recency selectors discard information the future query needs." **Compare:** Self-Guided TTT's evidence-span selector is a **learned model-guided** selector rather than an attention-magnitude heuristic — this is a candidate answer to the compaction failure mode. Watch for whether the two combine cleanly or whether TTT's LM-loss target diverges from what a rate-distortion-optimal compaction would target.
