### 3. ACC: Compiling Agent Trajectories for Long-Context Training
- **Source:** arXiv (cs.AI / cs.CL, submitted May 21, 2026)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.21850
- **Authors / org:** Qisheng Su, Zhen Fang, Shiting Huang, Yu Zeng, Yiming Zhao, Kou Shi, Ziao Zhang, Lin Chen, Zehui Chen, Lijun Wu, **Feng Zhao** (likely USTC / Shanghai AI Lab cluster based on co-author pattern)
- **Focus area:** Context engineering / Local & open models
- **What it is:** Standard agent SFT masks tool responses and only trains turn-level tool selection — the cross-turn evidence dependencies that long-context reasoning needs go unsupervised. **ACC (Agent Context Compilation)** converts trajectories from search / SWE / database-querying agents into **long-context QA pairs** that combine the original question with all tool responses + environment observations gathered across many turns, then **trains the model to answer directly without invoking tools**. Makes question↔evidence dependencies explicit; supervises long-context reasoning over distant context segments without additional annotation. **Headline result: training Qwen3-30B-A3B with ACC reaches 68.3 MRCR (+18.1) and 77.5 GraphWalks (+7.6) — comparable to Qwen3-235B-A22B** — while preserving GPQA / MMLU-Pro / AIME / IFEval. Mechanism analysis: task-adaptive attention restructuring and expert specialization emerge.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** Highest local-fit datapoint of the week. (a) Justin's M5 Max baseline is **Qwen3-30B-A3B at Q4_K_M** — ACC says that exact backbone can match 235B-A22B on cross-turn-coreference and graph-traversal long-context tasks. Both numerical gains are 8-18 points; **the +18.1 on MRCR is the largest publicly reported open-30B long-context gain of the May cluster**. (b) Repositions agent trajectories as a *training-data asset*, not just a logging artifact — every Justin-run MCP harness already generates this trajectory shape. The data is sitting on disk; ACC says fine-tune on it and the base model picks up long-context reasoning the harness was previously simulating. (c) Composes with [arxiv:2605.04050] LCM (Volt) — LCM compresses context at inference time inside the harness; ACC distills the cross-turn evidence into the weights at training time. Same axis, opposite ends.
- **Action signal:** `try-now` `local-fit` — wait for code release, then run ACC's recipe over Justin's own MCP-harness trajectory archive to fine-tune a Qwen3-30B-A3B variant tailored to his actual tool surface.

### 4. Maestro: RL Orchestration of Hierarchical Model-Skill Ensembles
- **Source:** arXiv (cs.AI, submitted May 21, 2026)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22177
- **Authors / org:** Jinyang Wu, Guocheng Zhai, Ruihan Jin, Yuhao Shen, Zhengxi Lu, Fan Zhang, Haoran Luo, Zheng Lian, Zhengqi Wen, **Jianhua Tao** (Tsinghua / CAS cluster — Jianhua Tao is a long-time multimodal LLM/speech author)
- **Focus area:** Agent orchestration
- **What it is:** Trains a **lightweight 4B policy** to dynamically compose ensembles of **frozen** expert models and a two-tier skill library, deciding at each step (a) whether to invoke an external expert, (b) which model-skill pair to select, (c) when to terminate. Headline: **70.1% average accuracy across 10 multimodal benchmarks vs. GPT-5 69.3% / Gemini-2.5-Pro 68.7%**; 59.5% on 4 OOD-expert benchmarks. The policy is the only trained component — all experts stay frozen.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** (a) **First open multimodal-benchmark result where a 4B orchestrator over frozen experts beats GPT-5** at the same task. Operationally relevant for any companion-app design that wants to hold cost low while passing through to frontier experts only when warranted — Justin's expected long-run cost shape. (b) Sits cleanly on the orchestration axis next to [arxiv:2605.05007] Uno-Orchestra (learned routing) and [arxiv:2605.15040] Orchard (open framework) — Maestro adds the *frozen-expert-ensemble* design point: the orchestrator is the only weight you ship/train, every other capability is a black-box API. (c) Pairs as `compare` with item #5 below — both attack "do orchestration cheaper" but from opposite directions (Maestro: small RL router atop frozen experts; Compiling Workflows: distill the orchestration logic *into* the worker's weights).
- **Action signal:** `track` `compare` — wait for code/weights release; the 4B-router-beats-GPT-5 claim should be reproduced before adopting.

### 5. Compiling Agentic Workflows into LLM Weights (Subterranean Agents)
- **Source:** arXiv (submitted May 21, 2026)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22502

- **Focus area:** Agent orchestration / Coding agents
- **What it is:** Direct frame against the framework-proliferation story (the authors cite "290,000+ combined GitHub stars" across orchestration frameworks). ### 6. Ratchet: A Minimal Hygiene Recipe for Self-Evolving LLM Agents
- **Source:** arXiv (submitted May 21, 2026)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22148
- **Authors / org:** Xing Zhang, Yanwei Cui, Guanghui Wang, Ziyuan Li, Wei Qiu, Bing Zhu, Peiyang He
- **Focus area:** Agent memory / Long-running autonomous loops
- **What it is:** Frames the self-evolving-agent problem as **skill *lifecycle* management, not skill *authoring***. Four mechanisms applied to a frozen LLM agent that writes / retrieves / curates / retires its own natural-language skills: (i) **outcome-driven retirement** (a skill that loses recently gets dropped); (ii) **bounded active-cap** (max number of active skills enforced); (iii) **meta-skill authoring guidance** (a skill that tells the agent how to write skills); (iv) **pattern canonicalisation** (collapse near-duplicates). Headline numbers on Claude Opus 4.7: **MBPP+ hard-100 Pass@1 from 0.258 ± 0.047 baseline → 0.584 rolling mean / 0.658 ± 0.042 peak across 100 rounds × 3 seeds**; rolling-mean lift **+0.328 ± 0.018 vs. +0.002 ± 0.005 no-skill control**; **+0.22 peak on SWE-bench Verified over 20 rounds**. Ablations: retirement + meta-skill authoring are load-bearing; explicit deduplication is subsumed.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** The ledger has been tracking [arxiv:2605.06614] SkillOS (curator-executor split, Markdown SkillRepo), [arxiv:2605.18401] SkillsVote (Collection / Search / Post-Execution gating, +7.9 pp Terminal-Bench / +2.6 pp SWE-Bench Pro), and [arxiv:2605.12978] consolidation-decay (54% of solved ARC-AGI problems re-fail after LLM-driven consolidation). Ratchet sharpens the cluster: (a) **outcome-driven retirement is the missing primitive in SkillOS** — SkillOS adds skills but rarely removes them, which is exactly the failure mode consolidation-decay names. (b) The **+0.328 rolling-mean** number on MBPP+ hard-100 with Claude Opus 4.7 is operationally large enough to consider implementing — Justin's MCP companion stack accumulates similar conversation / tool-use traces that already shadow a skill library. (c) Ablation finding "deduplication subsumed by meta-skill authoring" is the design lesson — a single skill that says "before writing a new skill, check what already exists" replaces a separate dedup pipeline.
- **Action signal:** `try-now` `compare` — implement Ratchet's four mechanisms over an existing SkillOS-shaped repository; A/B against current behaviour to test whether outcome-driven retirement closes the consolidation-decay gap.

### 7. IdleSpec: Exploiting Idle Time via Speculative Planning for LLM Agents
- **Source:** arXiv (submitted May 21, 2026)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22154
- **Authors / org:** Daewon Choi, Kyunghyun Park, Woomin Song (KAIST), Saket Dingliwal, Sai Muralidhar Jayanthi (Amazon AGI), Jinwoo Shin (KAIST), Aram Galstyan (Amazon AGI)
- **Focus area:** Tools & connectors / Inference-time compute
- **What it is:** Frames tool-call wait time as a free compute budget that current agentic harnesses waste. **IdleSpec** speculatively plans (and pre-executes where safe) during the tool-wait window; rolls back when observation arrives that contradicts the speculation. Scales across varying compute budgets per tool call and accounts for observation uncertainty. Headlines: **+5.1 pp on GAIA / FRAMES average with Gemini-2.5-Flash (55.6%)**, **up to +9.1 pp on MLE-Bench Any Medal rate**.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** Direct composition partner to [arxiv:2605.15077] AsyncFC (concurrency-via-symbolic-futures, UC Berkeley) — AsyncFC decouples decode from execution; IdleSpec uses the resulting idle window to do useful inference-time-compute work. Both are harness-side, no fine-tune. For Justin's MCP stack where most tool calls are MCP-server round-trips (~100ms–10s), the cumulative wait time per long-horizon agent run is large and currently wasted; IdleSpec is the first paper to put a benchmark number on closing this gap (+5.1 pp GAIA at the harness level is unusual). Pairs with [arxiv:2605.20630] IBM MCP-pipeline caching as a second harness-level latency optimization for the same MCP workload.
- **Action signal:** `try-now` — concept is harness-side and portable; implement an IdleSpec branch on the existing MCP runner and measure whether the GAIA-class +5.1 pp transfers to Justin's task mix.

### 8. ArborKV: Structure-Aware KV Cache for Tree-Based LLM Reasoning
- **Source:** arXiv (submitted May 21, 2026)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22106
- **Authors / org:** Yeqiu Chen, Ziyan Liu, Zhenxin Huang, Runquan Gui, Hong Wang, Lei Liu
- **Focus area:** Context engineering / Local & open models
- **What it is:** Tree-of-Thoughts (ToT) reasoning blows up KV memory because every branch keeps its own KV slab. ArborKV observes that near-term decoding depends primarily on the active branch + its ancestors, so it ships a **tree-aware allocation policy** + **lightweight value estimator** that drives **token-extractive eviction with lazy rehydration** to support back-tracking to a previously evicted branch. Headlines: **up to ~4× peak KV memory reduction with near-full-retention accuracy**.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** Joins the multi-paper KV cluster from last week (item-2 OScaR INT2 + item-3 OCTOPUS octahedral + item-4 Mix-Quant FP4-prefill) on a different axis: **KV memory specifically for tree-shaped reasoning patterns**. This is the codec for inference-time-compute methods that branch — OpenDeepThink Bradley-Terry, MAP, InsightReplay — all of which Justin would want to run locally on M5 Max. 4× peak KV reduction on a ToT pattern with Qwen3-30B-A3B is the difference between "branch budget 4" and "branch budget 16" on a 64 GB box. Composes orthogonally with item-2 OScaR from the May 21 digest (per-token KV codec) and [arxiv:2605.18643] ZEDA (active-expert reduction).
- **Action signal:** `track` `local-fit` — wait for code; queue alongside OScaR / OCTOPUS for a M5 Max + Qwen3-30B-A3B local benchmark when at least one of them releases an Apple Silicon port.

### 9. TerminalWorld: Benchmarking Agents on Real-World Terminal Tasks

- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22535
- **Authors / org:** Zhaoyang Chu, Jiarui Hu, Xingyu Jiang, Pengyu Zou, Han Li, Chao Peng, **Peter O'Hearn**, **Earl T. Barr**, **Mark Harman**, **Federica Sarro**, He Ye (UCL / Meta / Tsinghua cluster — O'Hearn, Barr, Harman, Sarro are UCL software-engineering / formal-methods authors)

### 10. NVIDIA Nemotron-Labs Diffusion Language Models (3B / 8B / 14B + 8B VLM)
- **Source:** Hugging Face Blog (NVIDIA, May 23, 2026)
- **Link:** https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/nemotron-labs-diffusion
- **Authors / org:** NVIDIA Nemotron-Labs
- **Focus area:** Frontier models / Local & open models / Inference-time compute
- **What it is:** Open family of **diffusion language models** that generate text by producing 32-token blocks in parallel and iteratively denoising them, rather than autoregressive token-by-token. (a) **Local fit on M5 Max** is conditional on SGLang or an MLX port being available; SGLang is the published path, MLX is not yet there. (b) **The self-speculation mode is structurally similar to AsyncFC and IdleSpec** from earlier items — three orthogonal latency optimizations (one in the decoder, two in the harness) all landing in the same week. (c) The +1.2% accuracy gain over Qwen3-8B is modest; the speedup is the headline. For Justin's companion-app token loops, a 4× tok/s lift at the 8B-class is the difference between "interactive" and "noticeably laggy" on his hardware.
