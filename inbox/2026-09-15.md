### 1. When Tool Calls Succeed but Workflows Fail: Anomalies at the Agent-Tool Boundary
- **Source:** arXiv (new)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15397
- **Authors/org:** class="authors"> Authors: Artem Trofimov, Boris Novikov
- **Focus area:** tools / MCP
- **What it is:** AI agents increasingly execute long-running workflows that externalize effects through independently supplied tools. Under retries, speculative execution, concurrency, and partial failures, the resulting external state may be inconsistent with the workflow's intended resolution: required effects may be missing or duplicated, aborted effects may survive, and committed effects may depend on provisional state that is later withdrawn.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** Tool-call success ≠ workflow success — instrument Mitamaton/MCP at workflow level, not just per-call OK rates.
- **Action signal:** `try-now`

## Conflicts & open questions

1. **Corrupt Plans / CoT monitors [2609.15989] vs ActGuard pre-exec audit [2609.14987]** — Visible CoT can be clean while plans are corrupt; action-level pre-exec may catch what trace monitors miss — or both can fail if tools are already compromised.
2. **Persistent memory poisoning [2609.13889] vs LIMBO lifelong memory [2609.14138]** — More durable inference-time memory increases attack surface unless writes are validated (AcquireBound/HazardAuditor-style).
3. **Bash-all-you-need [2609.11999] vs rich MCP/tool surfaces (Koa [2609.15066], tool-boundary anomalies [2609.15397])** — Shell-first simplicity vs structured tool authz and workflow-level correctness — Mitamaton likely needs both tiers.
4. **OrchSLM / SLM orchestration [2609.13470] vs ZGCM-1 compact tool-using 7B [2609.13356] vs Koa 120B tool specialist [2609.15066]** — When to route subtasks to local SLMs vs one strong tool model vs enterprise-scale post-train — cost/privacy/quality tradeoff still open.
5. **Stateful handoffs [2609.13800] + Recoverability [2609.13672] vs Identity-beyond-recall [2609.13637]** — Continuity can mean residual task state, crash recovery, or persona identity — these are related but not the same primitive.
6. **HarnessBandit multi-harness RL [2609.13739] vs Router-Within native skill routing [2609.15982] vs SkillLift rubric evolution [2609.15396]** — Optimize harness scheduling vs elicit routing from a frozen model vs evolve skill rubrics — where should Justin invest first?
