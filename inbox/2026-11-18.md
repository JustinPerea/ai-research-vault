### 1. KV-LTM: Persistent Per-Session KV Caches as Long-Term Memory for Personal Assistants
- **Source:** arXiv (new or cross-list)
- **Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21998
- **Authors/org:** Ziv Kfir, Ido Kfir
- **Focus area:** memory: persistent KV cache tier
- **What it is:** Proposes KV-LTM, a memory tier that stores each session's raw text plus its KV cache under a fixed reader model and answers by restoring cached sessions and appending the question. It is a design plus analytic sizing and an evaluation protocol, not measured results: per-token state is approximately 31K-328K bytes for the pinned configurations, about 0.34-3.6 TB per user-year versus approximately 49 MB of raw text.
- **Why it matters for Justin's agents:** Directly relevant to persistence across sessions for a personal assistant: text stays the record, the cache is only a prefill accelerator, but storage at 0.34-3.6 TB per user-year rules it out on a phone or a 64 GB Mac without a tight retention budget.
- **Action signal:** `track`

### 9. RAG in a Trenchcoat: When Minimal Memory Is Enough for Agentic Systems, and When It Isn't
